2. Data we process

  • Contact and inquiry data: name, email address, company, position and any content voluntarily included in the message.
  • Technical browsing data: IP address, date and time, requested resource, response code, browser and equivalent technical data that the server may record to operate and protect the website.
  • Professional relationship data: information needed to prepare a proposal, manage the contractual relationship, provide services, issue invoices and comply with legal obligations when the relationship progresses beyond a website inquiry.

We do not request special categories of data through the website. Avoid including sensitive information or confidential documents in an initial email unless essential and an agreed channel is in place.

4. Source and nature of the data

Inquiry data comes directly from the data subject. Providing data in an email is voluntary, but without the minimum information needed to understand the request, we may be unable to respond or prepare a proposal. If you provide another person’s data, you must be legally entitled to do so and inform them of this policy.

5. Recipients and processors

We do not sell personal data. Providers acting on Grimoni’s behalf may access it where necessary to provide and secure the services, principally:

  • Hetzner: infrastructure and hosting.
  • Microsoft 365: email, collaboration and storage associated with communications.
  • Professional advisers, financial institutions and public authorities where necessary to manage the relationship or comply with a legal obligation.

Grimoni requires processors to provide appropriate safeguards and limits their access to what is necessary to provide the service.

6. International transfers

The use of technology providers may involve processing data outside Andorra. Where the recipient is not located in a territory recognized as providing adequate protection, Grimoni will apply the safeguards required by Llei 29/2021 and its implementing rules, including the applicable contractual safeguards. You may request further information about the applicable mechanism by emailing info@grimoni.com.

7. Rights

You may request access to, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability of your data free of charge. Email info@grimoni.com, state the right you wish to exercise and provide only the information necessary to verify your identity. We will respond without undue delay and, as a general rule, within one month; where the request is complex, this period may be extended in accordance with the law and we will inform you accordingly.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Agència Andorrana de Protecció de Dades (APDA).

8. Automated decision-making and minors

Browsing and contacting us through this website do not result in decisions based solely on automated processing or profiling with legal or similarly significant effects. The website is intended for organizations and professionals, not for anyone under 16.

9. Security

We apply technical and organizational measures proportionate to the risks to prevent unauthorized access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No system is infallible; if you detect an incident relating to grimoni.com, report it to info@grimoni.com.

10. Cookies and changes

The website’s current configuration is described in the Cookie Policy. This policy may be updated if processing activities, providers or regulations change; the date of the current version will appear at the top.
