CAPABILITIES

The complete cycle
of an operation.

Each system includes only the capabilities needed to solve the target process.

01

Capture

Read information wherever it arrives, regardless of format or channel.

  • Email and messaging
  • PDFs, images and voice
  • ERP, CRM and databases
02

Understand

Interpret intent, entities, relationships and rules in the business context.

  • Classification and extraction
  • Entity resolution
  • Connected internal knowledge
03

Decide

Compare options, detect exceptions and propose the next step with evidence.

  • Prioritization
  • Risk prediction and detection
  • Confidence thresholds
04

Execute

Coordinate applications and teams to complete the authorized action.

  • Update systems
  • Generate documents and messages
  • Escalate to the appropriate person
05

Learn

Measure outcomes and turn human validation into continuous improvement.

  • Evaluation by use case
  • Decision traceability
  • Controlled review and adjustment
